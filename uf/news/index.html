Man found dead in underground Fresno laboratory; officials seek public's help with identification

By Marisol Vega, Ledger Staff Writer  ·  Published Aug. 15, 2026, 9:41 p.m.  ·  Updated Aug. 16, 2026, 7:12 a.m.
[ PHOTO UNAVAILABLE AT COUNTY'S REQUEST ]
County vehicles remained outside the site through Friday afternoon. Officials declined to allow photography of the facility entrance.

FRESNO — A man was found dead early Friday morning inside a below-ground laboratory facility in Fresno, county officials confirmed Saturday.

Emergency crews were dispatched shortly before 7 a.m. Friday and the man was pronounced dead at the scene by Fresno County EMS at approximately 7:30 a.m., according to a statement from the Fresno County Sheriff's Office. The statement said deputies found no evidence of forced entry and no visible signs of trauma.

Authorities have not identified the man, and the Fresno County Medical Examiner's Office said Saturday that efforts to establish his identity have so far been unsuccessful. The office is asking anyone with information about a missing man between the ages of 35 and 50, approximately 5 feet 10 inches tall, to come forward.

"At this time we are treating this as an unattended death, pending the medical examiner's findings. We are asking for the public's patience — and the public's help." — Sgt. R. Ibarra, Fresno County Sheriff's Office

Questions also surround the facility itself. The Ledger was unable to locate business filings, permits, or utility records identifying an owner or operator of the laboratory, which sits below street level and is not visible from the road. Two workers at a neighboring property said Friday they had been unaware the facility existed.

A postmortem examination was conducted Saturday morning. A spokesperson for the medical examiner's office said the cause of death remains under investigation and that preliminary findings "will not be released at this time."

The sheriff's office declined to say what was recovered from the scene, citing the open investigation.

This is a developing story and will be updated.

Have information? The Fresno County Sheriff's Office asks anyone with information regarding this incident, or the identity of the deceased, to contact the non-emergency line and reference case number 26-08-1447.
